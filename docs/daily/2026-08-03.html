English summary

This study examined the impact of sex chromosome complement on cognitive function and neuroinflammation in mouse models of Alzheimer's disease (AD). By generating various sex chromosome genotypes (XX, XO, XY, XXY), the researchers demonstrated that XY mice exhibited greater cognitive impairment and higher IL-17A levels compared to XX and XXY mice. These differences were mediated by X chromosome dosage rather than Y chromosome effects, while amyloid-β and tau pathologies remained unaffected.

要点：コンピュータ解析により、FDA承認薬であるフラボキサートが、優れた血液脳関門透過性を持つ新規NLRP3インフラマソーム阻害剤候補として同定された。

English summary

This computational drug repurposing study screened 2,600 FDA-approved compounds to identify novel NLRP3 inflammasome inhibitors for Alzheimer's disease therapy. Flavoxate was identified as the most promising candidate, demonstrating high docking scores, stable binding affinity in molecular dynamics simulations, and favorable blood-brain barrier permeability. The findings suggest that repurposing flavoxate could offer a clinically translatable strategy to mitigate NLRP3-mediated neuroinflammation.
