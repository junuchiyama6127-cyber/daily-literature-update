English summary

This review highlights the pathogenic role and therapeutic potential of HMGB1 signaling in Alzheimer's disease (AD). Pro-inflammatory disulfide HMGB1 interacts with RAGE and TLR4 receptors, driving multiple downstream cascades that promote neuroinflammation, amyloid-β accumulation, and tau hyperphosphorylation. Targeting HMGB1-mediated neuroinflammatory pathways represents a promising therapeutic strategy for mitigating AD pathology.
